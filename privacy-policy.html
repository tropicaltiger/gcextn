Privacy Policy — GBP Review Assistant Last updated: 06-08-2026 GBP Review Assistant ("we," "our," "the app") helps local business owners manage and respond to reviews on their Google Business Profile using AI-drafted replies. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can control it. 1. Information we collect Account information When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (your name and email address) to identify your account. Google Business Profile data With your permission (via Google OAuth), we access: Your business profile information (name, address, category) Customer reviews left on your profile The ability to post replies to reviews on your behalf, only after you approve them (unless you've enabled auto-post) We do not access or store any other Google account data beyond what's needed for these features. Usage data We may collect basic usage information (e.g., which features you use, how often you log in) to improve the app. 2. How we use your information To fetch new reviews from your connected Google Business Profile To generate AI-drafted reply suggestions using a third-party AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) To post approved replies back to your Google Business Profile To send you notifications about new reviews (email or WhatsApp, if enabled) To improve and maintain the app We do not sell your data. We do not use your review data or business information for advertising purposes. 3. Third-party services We use the following third-party services to operate the app: Google Business Profile API — to read reviews and post replies Anthropic Claude API — to generate draft reply text. Review content is sent to this service solely to generate a reply suggestion; it is not used to train AI models. [Hosting provider, e.g. Render/Railway] — to run our backend servers Each of these providers has its own privacy practices, and we only share the minimum data necessary for them to perform their function. 4. Data retention We retain review data and draft replies for as long as your account is active, so you can view your reply history. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6). 5. Data security We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. 6.
